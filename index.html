We started The Strand Studio because getting your hair done shouldn't feel like waiting at the DMV. Every appointment starts with a conversation, runs on time, and ends with a plan for how to look after your hair at home.

  • One client per stylistNo double booking, so your stylist is with you the whole time.
  • Honest timingWe tell you how long it will take and what it will cost before we start.
  • All hair typesEvery stylist is trained in curly and coily cutting, not just one.
  • Gentle productsLow-ammonia color and sulfate-free care, chosen for your hair.
