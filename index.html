2140 E 4th Street, Long Beach

The Strand started with two stylists who were tired of rushed appointments and surprise prices. So we built the salon we wanted to work in: six chairs, big west-facing windows and one client per stylist, so your stylist is with you the whole time.

Every visit starts with a conversation about how you actually wear your hair, not just how it looks walking out the door. We tell you the time and the price before we begin, every stylist is trained in curly and coily cutting, and we use low-ammonia color and sulfate-free care.

You'll leave with a plan for your hair at home, and usually a coffee in hand.
